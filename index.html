Scope of access

You choose the channel.
You control the action.

The app requests permission to manage your YouTube account for channel management. Depending on the operation supported by the YouTube API, this permission can allow reading channel and content information, uploading videos, and creating, editing, or deleting videos, playlists, comments, and other supported resources.

Changes are made only in response to the operator’s instructions. Google authorization grants access; it does not itself upload, edit, or delete content. Channel access depends on the permissions granted by Google and YouTube.

Intended audience

Personal use

This app is intended for its operator’s own authorized channels. Public registration and access for unrelated third parties are not offered.

Where data lives

On the local device

Authorization credentials and channel records are kept locally. This information website does not receive OAuth tokens or channel data.

Your controls

Revoke and delete

Remove access in Google account permissions and remove locally stored data using the process in the privacy policy.

About this website

These pages explain the desktop app and its policies. There is no sign-in or channel management interface on this site. Kingpin Channel Manager is independently operated and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or represented as verified by Google or YouTube.
