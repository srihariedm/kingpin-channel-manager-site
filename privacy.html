Privacy policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Kingpin Channel Manager is a personal desktop app operated through ytkingpin99@gmail.com. It uses YouTube API Services to connect and manage channels that the operator is authorized to access.

Information the app accesses

With your Google authorization, the app can access YouTube channel identifiers and titles, channel and video metadata, playlists, comments, and other resources needed for an operation you request. Management permission can include private information available to the authorized channel. Content and metadata that you choose to upload or change are sent to YouTube to perform that action.

The app stores OAuth access or refresh tokens, granted scopes, channel identifiers and titles, and authorization metadata on the local Windows device. Local input files and operational output may also contain channel information. The app does not collect or store your Google account password; sign-in happens with Google.

How information is used

Credentials are used to maintain authorized access. Channel records identify the destination for your requested operations. Retrieved information is used to display or process the YouTube operation you request, and user-selected files and metadata are used for that operation. The app does not use Google or YouTube data for advertising, profiling, sale, or model training.

Storage, security, and retention

The desktop app has no hosted user-data backend. Its credential store uses Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) encryption for tokens. Channel records and other local files may be readable to someone with access to the device or Windows account. Device access, operating-system security, and any copies or backups remain under the operator’s control. No security measure guarantees complete protection.

Tokens are kept while the channel connection is needed and remains authorized. Stored API information must be refreshed or removed when it is no longer needed. Disconnecting a channel requires removing its associated credentials and locally retained API records. A Google revocation alone does not erase local files or their backups.

Sharing and external services

The app communicates with Google and YouTube to authenticate, refresh access, and execute authorized API requests. It does not sell data or provide channel data to advertisers, analytics providers, or unrelated third parties. The operator does not offer public user accounts or an external data-sharing service.

Google and YouTube process information under their own terms and policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

Revoke access and delete local data

For an individual channel, use Disconnect options beside that channel in the local app. Review the selected channel and choose one of these actions:

  • Remove locally: removes the channel’s encrypted token bundle and its local connection record. The Google authorization remains active.
  • Revoke at Google and remove locally: asks Google to revoke access, then removes the channel’s local credentials and connection record. Google revocation may also affect other channel authorizations granted to the same project by the same Google account. Review that impact before confirming.

You can also remove access in Google account permissions by selecting Kingpin Channel Manager. Repeat for each relevant Google account. Revocation there does not erase local files; also remove the affected local records using the app.

To remove all locally stored app data, first stop the app. In the local youtube-auth folder, delete credentials/client_secret.json, the encrypted token files matching private/*.dpapi, and status.json. Also remove any API output files, exported records, and copies or backups you created. Use the app for individual-channel removal; do not manually edit encrypted token files. Removing these files alone does not revoke Google authorization.

For deletion assistance, contact ytkingpin99@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, client secrets, or tokens by email. Deleting app data does not delete videos, comments, or other resources already saved on YouTube; manage those resources directly in YouTube or through a separately requested, authorized action.

This information website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no sign-in, forms, analytics scripts, advertising, embedded videos, or app-operated cookies. It does not receive desktop OAuth credentials or channel records. GitHub may process ordinary request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Questions and changes

Contact ytkingpin99@gmail.com for privacy questions, complaints, or deletion assistance. Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date. Any material new use of authorized data requires appropriate notice and consent before it begins.
